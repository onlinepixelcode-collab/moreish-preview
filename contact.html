FAQ

Good questions.

How do I order?

Fill your box in The Parlour, or design a cake in Make It Moreish, then send it to us on WhatsApp. We reply with a quote and payment details.

Do I need to pay a deposit?

Yes. No booking is secured until a 50% non-refundable deposit or full payment is received. If you're paying the remaining 50%, please pay via EFT before collection or delivery.

Do you deliver?

We're based in Johannesburg. You can collect, or we can arrange delivery. Tell us your area and date when you order.

How should I store my cheesecake?

Keep it frozen until serving, then let it stand for 3–5 minutes. Once it's been collected or delivered it's in your hands, so straight into the freezer!

Allergies?

Our desserts contain dairy, gluten and egg, and may contain traces of nuts. Please ask us about specific dietary needs before ordering.

How far ahead should I book a live station?

As early as you can, especially for wedding and Eid season. Send your date and we'll confirm availability.
