Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Draft for review. This template was written for the Just Moreish website and should be checked by the business (and ideally a legal adviser) before going live.

Just Moreish ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect when you use this website, order from us or book a live station, and how we look after it in line with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

1. What we collect

  • Details you choose to send us: your name, phone number, email address, delivery area, event details and order notes.
  • Messages you send us on WhatsApp, by email or through Instagram.
  • Basic technical information your browser shares when you visit (for example device type and pages viewed), if analytics are switched on.

2. How the website works

Your box (cart), theme choice and similar preferences are saved only in your own browser (local storage) so the site remembers them. They are not sent to us. When you tap Send order on WhatsApp or submit an enquiry form, the website opens WhatsApp with a message you can read and edit before you send it. Nothing is sent until you press send in WhatsApp.

The Ask Moreish assistant runs entirely in your browser. Your questions are not stored or sent to us or to any third party.

3. Why we use your information

  • To prepare quotes, confirm orders, arrange collection or delivery, and plan events.
  • To contact you about your order or booking.
  • To meet our legal and accounting obligations.

We do not sell your personal information. We will only send you marketing messages if you have agreed to receive them, and you can opt out at any time.

4. Who we share it with

Only with service providers we need to fulfil your order (for example delivery partners or payment providers) and only as much as they need. Messages you send through WhatsApp, email or Instagram are also processed by those platforms under their own privacy policies.

5. How long we keep it

We keep order and booking information for as long as needed to provide the service and to meet legal record-keeping requirements, after which it is deleted or anonymised.

6. Keeping it safe

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information against loss, misuse and unauthorised access.

7. Your rights

Under POPIA you may ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to its use. You may also lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa.

8. Contact

For any privacy question or request, email justmoreish@gmail.com or WhatsApp 064 505 8652.
