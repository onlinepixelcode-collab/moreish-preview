1. Ordering & quotes

Orders are placed by WhatsApp or email. Sending your box or a custom cake design from this website is a request for a quote, not a confirmed order. We'll reply with a quote, which may differ from the guide prices shown on the website.

2. Deposits & payment

  • No booking is secured until a 50% non-refundable deposit or full payment is received.

  • Prices are in South African Rand and may change without notice until your order is confirmed.

3. Collection & delivery

Collection times and delivery areas are agreed when you order. Please be on time for collection or available to receive your delivery, as our products are frozen.

4. Storage & responsibility

Our ice-cream cheesecakes, ice-cream cakes and gelato must be kept frozen. We are not held responsible once your order has been collected or delivered. Let cheesecakes stand for 3–5 minutes before serving.

5. Allergens

Our products contain dairy, gluten and egg and may contain traces of nuts. It is your responsibility to tell us about allergies and dietary needs before ordering.

Custom designs (type, base, flavour, toppings, size and message) are made to order. The 3D preview on our website is an illustration; the real cake is handmade and may look slightly different. Branded toppings depend on availability and may be substituted with a similar alternative after checking with you.

Deposits are non-refundable. If you need to move your date, contact us as soon as possible and we'll do our best to help.
